Hello, I'm Michelle. Muddy Paws is my mobile dog grooming salon — a fully equipped, self-contained van that parks up outside your home and pampers your pet on the spot.

At the heart of the van is the latest hydrobath technology: a deep-cleansing massage bath your dog will genuinely enjoy, with shampoos individually chosen to suit their coat and skin. Then it's styling, clipping, nails and ears — all with kind, caring, individual attention.

There's no noisy salon, no cages and no queue of other dogs. It's one client at a time, steps from your front door — and no special trip for you at all.

The honest small print, so there are no surprises on spa day.

Where do you cover?

We're based in Framfield, near Uckfield, East Sussex and travel to homes across the surrounding villages. Give Michelle a ring to check your road — if we can park up nearby, we'll come to you.

What's on board the van?

Everything. The van is fully equipped and self-contained — warm water, power, the latest hydrobath, dryers and a grooming table. We just need a spot to park close to your home.

Why mobile instead of a salon?

No cages, no queues, no noisy salon full of strange dogs. Your pup gets one-to-one attention for the whole appointment, steps from their own front door — brilliant for nervous, elderly or first-time dogs. And there's no special trip for you.

What's a hydrobath?

A deep-cleansing massage bath — warm water and shampoo are massaged right through the coat, so dogs come out cleaner, calmer and smelling wonderful. Shampoos are chosen individually for your dog's coat and skin.

How do I book?

Just call Michelle on 07910 835962 or 01825 890061, or send an email. Every groom is tailored to your dog, so we'll chat about breed, coat and what you'd like done.
